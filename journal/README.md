# Journal

Raw, dated learnings captured while fresh. Low friction on purpose: a trigger, a takeaway, whatever evidence exists, and a next step. Entries are never edited after the fact except to fill in `graduated_to` when they are distilled into an area learning.

Naming: `journal/YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`. Template: `templates/journal.md`.
