# Engineering Manager: position description

Source document, anonymized. Written 2025-07. See [Engineering managers](../engineering-managers.md)
for how it is used.

## Mission

The Engineering Manager is responsible for enabling the success, performance, and well-being
of their team. Their mission is to help the team deliver meaningful outcomes by creating the
right environment for autonomy, alignment, and growth, balancing day-to-day execution with
long-term health and sustainability.

## Key responsibilities

- **Grow people.** Support engineers through regular 1:1s, feedback, and coaching. Guide
  career development and help align personal and company goals.
- **Own execution.** Partner with Product and Design to plan, prioritize, and execute work.
  Drive clarity in scope, expectations, and timelines.
- **Ensure technical implementation.** Oversee the quality, maintainability, and scalability
  of the team's technical output. Participate in architecture discussions, RFC reviews, and
  design feedback.
- **Foster a healthy team environment.** Create psychological safety, resolve conflicts, and
  ensure a balanced workload. Monitor engagement, morale, and development.
- **Make pragmatic tradeoffs.** Help the team navigate tech debt, velocity, and quality. Push
  back when timelines or designs introduce long-term risks.
- **Prevent bottlenecks.** Ensure knowledge is shared, responsibilities are distributed, and
  the team doesn't depend on a single person, including yourself.

## Boundaries

- It is **not a tech lead role**: deep technical direction is driven in partnership with
  Staff Engineers and senior team members.
- It is **not a coding role**: technical contribution comes through review, guidance, and
  context-sharing, not critical-path delivery.
- It is **not a solo role**: team and product success are achieved through collaboration
  across disciplines.

## Cross-functional collaboration

Engineering Managers work closely with Product, Design, Operations, and other Engineering
Managers to ensure the team is focused and aligned. They partner with Staff Engineers and
ICs to maintain a high bar for technical quality while driving team delivery and business
outcomes.
